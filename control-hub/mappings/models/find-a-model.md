# Find a Model

Within models you can see all of the models that have been created. They are grouped by the business area they are assigned. You can select the business area to see all of its models or you can use the filters to narrow down your results.

## **Business Areas**

All models sit in a business area and you can select the business area to see the models within it.

## Filtering Your Models

You can apply filters to show models based on their status, type or keywords. When you filter your models you will only see the business areas with matching results in them.&#x20;

***

## Edit

Select the edit button of the model you want to edit. You can change any of the details just like for new models.

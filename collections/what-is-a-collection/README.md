# What is a Collection?

When you perform a search you can save items from your results into a space for you, your colleagues, or for a SAR response. By grouping data as you go, you can come back to it any time.&#x20;

Creating a collection doesn't duplicate the item, but creates a way to access it more quickly.

## Finding your collections:

You can access your collections from anywhere in Aiimi Insight Engine.&#x20;

1. Select Collections from the top navigator.

From here you can see all your collections including those shared with you. You can filter these by Modified Date, Who modified it or the number of results in a collection.

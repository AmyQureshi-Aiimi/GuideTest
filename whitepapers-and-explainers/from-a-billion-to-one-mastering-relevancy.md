# From a Billion To One – Mastering Relevancy

Aiimi Insight Engine provides organisations the ability to index their entire information estate. Everything from unstructured SharePoint documents to CRM data, and even operational data in event management systems. Indexing a huge volume and variety of information requires consideration around relevancy so users are not overwhelmed with results.

The following sections help administrators with the fundamentals to support good relevancy and the configurations needed to deliver a good user experience. Taking users to precisely what they need, when they need it.

We are going to cover:

* Setting up labels to structure your information.
* Populating labels, and other enrichment steps.
* Configuring experiences based on users persona and what they are trying to achieve.

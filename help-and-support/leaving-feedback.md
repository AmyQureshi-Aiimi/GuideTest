# Leaving Feedback

We want to hear your ideas, feedback and comments about Workplace AI. Administrators review and organise feedback to help make the experience better where possible.

You can access the feedback area from every page of Workplace AI.&#x20;

1. At the bottom of every page is a Give Feedback option.&#x20;
   * A pop up will open for you to rate and share your experience to help improve it for others.
2. Select the number of stars you would rate your experience.
3. If you are having a problem you can add more detail about your feedback.
   * Select the Related Page from the dropdown.&#x20;
   * Enter any Related search terms that you used during an experience.
4. You can enter any comments into the free text box Have you say.
5. Select Submit.

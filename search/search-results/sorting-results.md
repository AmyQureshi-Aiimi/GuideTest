# Sorting Results

You can sort your results by selecting the "Sort by" dropdown at the top of the screen.&#x20;

<table><thead><tr><th width="155">Sort By</th><th>Definition</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Relevance</td><td>Order the results list in numerical order by relevancy score. (0-9) </td></tr><tr><td>Extension</td><td>Order the results list in alphabetical order by the extension. (A-Z) </td></tr><tr><td>Owner</td><td>Order the results list in alphabetical order by owners name. (A-Z) </td></tr><tr><td>Modified Date</td><td>Order the results list in ascending or descending order by modified date.</td></tr><tr><td>Created Date</td><td>Sort results in ascending or descending order by created date.</td></tr><tr><td>File Name</td><td>Sort and group results in alphabetical order by name. (A-Z) </td></tr><tr><td>File Size</td><td>Sort and group results in numerical order by size. (0-9)</td></tr></tbody></table>
